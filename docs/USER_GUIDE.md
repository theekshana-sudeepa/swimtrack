# SwimTrack User Guide

Welcome to **SwimTrack**, your personal swimming attendance control room!

## 1. Marking Attendance on Pool Deck
The Attendance Command Center (`/attendance`) is optimized for mobile touch targets and rapid desktop keyboard entry:

### Mobile Touch Controls
- **Tap Present** (Seafoam): Swimmer is on deck and swimming.
- **Tap Late** (Amber): Swimmer arrived after warmup.
- **Tap Absent** (Coral): Swimmer missed practice without excuse.
- **Tap Excused** (Sky Blue): Swimmer informed in advance (excluded from attendance rate calculations).

### Desktop Keyboard Shortcuts
Navigate with `ArrowDown` / `ArrowUp` (or `j` / `k`):
- `P`: Mark Present
- `L`: Mark Late
- `A`: Mark Absent
- `E`: Mark Excused
- `U`: Reset to Unmarked
- `Ctrl+Z` / `Cmd+Z`: Undo last change

### Bulk Actions
- **Mark All Present**: Fast-marks all participants present with celebratory confetti.
- **Remaining Absent**: Marks remaining unmarked attendees as absent with confirmation.

## 2. Managing Swimmers
Navigate to `/swimmers`:
- Click **Add Swimmer** to register friends with optional nickname, email, and phone.
- Filter between Active and Archived members.
- Click **Export CSV** to download attendance summaries.
- Click on any swimmer to view their profile, attendance streak, and personal heatmap.

## 3. Scheduling Practices & Series
Navigate to `/sessions`:
- Click **New Session** for one-off practices.
- Click **Recurring Series** to generate weekly practices automatically.
- Click **.ICS** on any session card to add the event to Apple Calendar or Google Calendar.
